More than a barbershop

Proper cuts
Proper chat

Concrete Barbers is a barbershop on Pontygwindy Industrial Estate in Caerphilly. We specialise in quality haircuts, fresh fades and sharp beard grooming. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead is recommended.

There's a pool table and a lounge for while you wait. Most people come for the cut and stay for the chat. We're a community as much as a barbershop.
